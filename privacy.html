Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy covers Maber Brain, a private internal tool operated by Maber Construction LLC ("we", "us") and used only by our own staff.

What data we access

With authorization from our QuickBooks Online administrator, Maber Brain accesses our own company's QuickBooks Online data: for example customers and projects, vendors, accounts, products and services, and bills. It also keeps the names and messaging IDs of the staff members who use it, and a record of each approval or rejection (who, when, and exactly what was approved).

How we use it

  • To prepare internal reports and summaries for our staff.
  • To create bills in QuickBooks, only after a staff member approves each one.
  • To keep an audit trail of those approvals.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with third parties. It is handled only by the service providers that run the tool for us (database hosting and the messaging service that delivers approval requests to our staff), solely to operate Maber Brain.

AI training

We do not use QuickBooks data to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not provide it to anyone for that purpose.

Security and retention

Access credentials are stored encrypted. Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the tool and for our business records. Disconnecting Maber Brain from QuickBooks stops all further access.
