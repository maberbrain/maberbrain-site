Maber Brain

An internal tool of Maber Construction LLC

Maber Brain is a private, internal operations tool used only by the staff of Maber Construction LLC, a general contractor in South Florida. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

What it does with QuickBooks Online:

  • Reads Maber Construction's own accounting data (for example customers, projects, vendors, accounts, products and services, and bills) to prepare internal reports.
  • Creates a bill only after a Maber Construction staff member has reviewed and approved that specific bill. Every approval and rejection is recorded.

Connecting to QuickBooks is done by Maber Construction's QuickBooks administrator from inside the company. If you reached this page and you don't work at Maber Construction, there is nothing here for you to connect.
